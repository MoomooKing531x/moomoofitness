# ELO Points Reference

This document shows how much ELO you earn for 10 reps/units of each exercise.

## Upper Body (Reps)

| Exercise | 10 Reps = ELO | Per Rep |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| Handstand Push-ups | 150 | 15 |
| Muscle-ups | 120 | 12 |
| Pull-ups | 70 | 7 |
| Dips | 70 | 7 |
| Neutral-grip Pull-ups | 30 | 3 |
| Chin-ups | 60 | 6 |
| Pike Push-ups | 40 | 4 |
| Push-ups | 30 | 3 |

## Lower Body (Reps)

| Exercise | 10 Reps = ELO | Per Rep |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| Pistol Squats | 80 | 8 |
| Bulgarian Split Squats | 40 | 4 |
| Jump Squats | 30 | 3 |
| Squats | 20 | 2 |

## Core (Reps)

| Exercise | 10 Reps = ELO | Per Rep |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| Toes-to-Bar | 70 | 7 |
| Hanging Leg Raises | 50 | 5 |
| Sit-ups | 20 | 2 |

## Core & Isometric (Seconds)

| Exercise | 10 Seconds = ELO | Per Second |
|----------|-------------------|------------|
| Handstand Hold | 40 | 4 |
| L-Sit | 35 | 3.5 |
| Hollow Body Hold | 20 | 2 |
| Plank | 15 | 1.5 |
| Wall Sit | 15 | 1.5 |
| Dead Hang | 10 | 1 |

## Activities (Distance)

| Exercise | 1 KM = ELO | Per KM |
|----------|------------|--------|
| Running | 120 | 120 |

## Notes:
- All exercises use the same ELO calculation regardless of how you log them (normal insert, recent exercises, or daily challenge)
- Exercise name variations (e.g., "Neutral-grip Pull-ups" vs "neutral-grip pull-ups") are normalized to match the same multiplier
- Unknown exercises default to 0.5 ELO per rep/second
